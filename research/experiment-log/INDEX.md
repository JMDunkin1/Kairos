## Lessons to carry forward

- Higher return from more market/leverage exposure is useful paper context, but not by itself timing alpha. Compare actual leveraged funds and frozen static allocations.
- Avoid promoting a validation winner after its later performance collapses; preserve the failure, costs and exposure rather than deleting it.
- Different model-test outputs on familiar market dates are still retrospective. Parameter resweeps do not create new evidence.
- Forecast accuracy for physical commodity prices is not executable trading profit. Missing quote/contract data means data-blocked, not a failed economic hypothesis.
- A retry should specify changed information, source availability, execution contract or economic mechanism, link the prior ID, freeze its finite budget, and reserve prospective dates honestly.
