# Experiment log contract

`experiments.jsonl` is append-only. Identify the latest record by `experiment_id` and the largest `revision`; every earlier event remains evidence. A changed record appends a revision. An identical import is idempotent. `experiments.csv`, `INDEX.md`, and the study pages are derived current views.

Study records own sources, frozen protocols, universe, selection, execution, accounting, exposure and audits. Design and configuration records inherit these through `parent_id`, `study_id`, or `source_inherited_from`; inheritance must be resolved before interpreting a rule. Configuration IDs preserve the original catalog ID. Gas version links use `parent_experiment_id` and `supersedes_metric_version`, and do not count as new independent hypotheses.

`mechanical_scenarios.jsonl` records retained development and released cost/delay/window/accounting rows and comparators, with source CSV, SHA256 and zero-based row index. These evaluations are not new economic discoveries. Final scope follows each frozen study: earlier batches evaluated selected configurations; the external and relationship batches predeclare descriptive final results for every fixed configuration, with nominations locked during development. An uncomputed model output does not make familiar underlying market dates pristine.

Counts distinguish completed/released equity configurations from development-only configurations with a closed final gate. Registered, source-gated and forward-only ideas are unrun. `LOG_AUDIT` is an immutable earlier checkpoint; `LOG_UPDATE_AUDIT` records a later independent snapshot and does not erase earlier audit evidence.

`equity_configs_in_completed_released_studies` counts every development configuration in a released study; `equity_individually_final_evaluated_configs` counts configurations actually evaluated on final data, including predeclared descriptive nonnominees. `equity_nominated_individually_final_evaluated_configs` counts only development-locked nominees. The legacy `equity_final_released_configs` field is retained as a deprecated alias of the first field, not a claim of individual final coverage. Earlier studies have 174 development configurations but only 18 selected configurations with final outputs (the limited study evaluates each selected rule on two assets).

A `study_version` links an implementation repair to the unchanged economic rules and original failed study. Schema-repaired configuration records preserve `original_config_id`; count unique economic rules once across versions, while retaining every execution and failure. A `portfolio_diagnostic` records fixed mixes of already released ledgers. Its sleeve profiles and repeated metric windows are diagnostics, not independent strategy discoveries or clean validation. Exposure proxies retain their stated scope and do not imply dynamic realized beta.

Four niche-market lanes and the older gas searches currently have study-level imports with exact linked catalogs and aggregate released evidence. Their historical per-configuration records have not been fully expanded into this log. Gas search budgets overlap; no additive lifetime strategy count is claimed. Summary source status is evidence availability, not independent recomputation of every old model.

Human summaries interpret the recorded evidence without modifying it. Unverified source copies, changing READMEs, versions outside release manifests, data-blocked candidates and unopened protected GEFS targets are identified in the history import. No protected targets, raw forecasts or unreleased holdouts are included.

Append new studies before outcomes and include a finite budget, changed mechanism, data availability, dates already exposed, execution and selection criteria. Later failures remain in the history. Archive and Library copies retain their study identity. `Libraryidentity.json` records the current readable report's exact Library ID and version for guarded replacement.
